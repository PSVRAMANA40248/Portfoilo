image(1).png WHAT ARE THSES Those are your navigation menu items (navbar tabs/buttons) in the portfolio header: Home → Main landing page About → Your personal introduction / profile section Skills → Technical + soft skills section Projects → Your project showcase Special Talent → Extra strengths (leadership, creativity, certifications, achievements) Contact → Ways to reach you (WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, Email) Why “About” looks different: The About tab has a border box around it, which means it’s likely the currently active/selected page. In your code, this is usually controlled by: nav a.active { border: 2px solid black; } If you want the active tab to look like Home instead: Use: nav a.active { color: #4c56ff; border-bottom: 2px solid #4c56ff; } Simple meaning: These are basically your website’s menu buttons used to move between sections/pages.
